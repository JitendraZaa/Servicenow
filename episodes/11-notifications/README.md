# Episode 11: Notifications

Paths: A D

In this episode you make ServiceNow write to the caller when her incident is resolved, and then check the message it produced. Priya reports a problem and hears nothing for 2 days, so she calls, and Sam answers the same call again and again. Telling the caller by hand is 1 more task that gets forgotten. A notification is a rule that writes a message when something happens to a record, so the same wording is generated at the right moment, by itself. If you know Salesforce, a notification is close to an email alert: an email generated by an automated process and sent to its recipients.

New terms: notification, condition, recipient, preview, email log.

## Before you start

- You finished [episode 10](../10-knowledge). Priya Nair exists and has an email address (priya.nair@example.com).
- Sign in as `admin`.
- The notification list can take a while to load the first time.

## Steps

1. Open the All menu and type `Notifications` in the filter: the menu lists every match, including portal, mobile and push ones. That is too broad, so clear the filter with the x and type `System Notification` instead. Under System Notification and Email choose Notifications. The list shows the ready-made rules, each with a name, the table it watches and its conditions. Click New.
2. Name it `Arcwell: incident resolved`. For Table, open the list, type `incident` in its search box and choose Incident. Choose the table before you submit, or the form shows a mandatory fields banner.
3. Open the When to send tab. Send when is already set to when a record is inserted or updated. Tick Updated. For the condition choose the field Incident state, the operator `is changed to`, and the value Resolved. The rule generates the message when the state of an incident changes to Resolved.
4. Open the What it will contain tab and fill it in:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Subject | Your incident ${number} is resolved |
   | Message | Hello, your incident has been resolved. Here is what was done. |

   After the sentence, use the field picker on the right to insert `${close_notes}` (Resolution notes) and then `${short_description}` (Short description). Each picked field becomes a gap that ServiceNow fills with that incident's own value. In Salesforce, merge fields play that role in an email template.
5. Click Submit, then open the notification again and click Preview Notification. The dialog picks an existing incident for you (INC0000060, a ready-made record in the video). The subject shows its number and the body shows its resolution notes and short description as real values.
6. Say who receives the message. Open Notifications again, search the list for Arcwell and open your notification. Open the Who will receive tab. Under Users/Groups in fields, unlock with the padlock, choose Caller, move it to Selected and click Update. The list now shows `caller_id`, the Caller field of the incident.
7. Create an incident: All menu, `Incident`, Create New.

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Caller | Priya Nair |
   | Category | Hardware |
   | Short description | Second monitor flickering |

   Click Submit (INC0010012 in the video).
8. Open the incident, go to the Resolution Information tab, set Resolution code to Solution provided and Resolution notes to `Replaced the display cable`. Click Resolve. The list shows a banner that the incident has been resolved.
9. Check what the notification produced. Open the All menu and under System Logs choose Emails. Filter the Subject for `Your incident`. 1 row remains: recipient priya.nair@example.com, subject `Your incident INC0010012 is resolved`. Email sending is turned off on this instance, so no message leaves; the Emails list shows the message the instance generated.
10. Open the row. Target is the incident. The Originating Event and Notification list names your rule, Arcwell: incident resolved. The Subject and Recipients repeat what you set, and the Body holds the real values: Resolution notes `Replaced the display cable` and Short description `Second monitor flickering`.

Result: a rule, a condition, a recipient and a message, checked in the email log.

## Your turn

1. Open `Arcwell: incident resolved`, go to What it will contain, and type the subject again with the priority after the number: `Your incident ${number} ${priority} is resolved`. Click Preview Notification (it works before saving; the subject shows the priority as `3 - Moderate`). Close the preview and retype the subject exactly as `Your incident ${number} is resolved`. Do not click Update.
2. Create an incident for Priya Nair with the short description `PRACTICE notification`, resolve it (Resolution code and Resolution notes, then Resolve), and open its row in All, System Logs, Emails (filter the Subject for `Your incident`). Then delete the practice incident (open it, Delete, confirm).

## Clean up

Make sure the subject is exactly `Your incident ${number} is resolved`. Delete the practice incident. The practice email row stays in the log; logs are not deleted. Keep `Arcwell: incident resolved` and INC0010012.
