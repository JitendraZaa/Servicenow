# Episode 10: Knowledge articles

Paths: U A D

In this episode you write the Wi-Fi fix once as a knowledge article, publish it, and find it as Priya. Sam fixed Priya's Wi-Fi problem on Monday. On Thursday three more engineers call with the same problem and Sam explains it again, because the fix lives in his head and in a resolved incident that nobody can search. A knowledge article is one page that answers one question, so it can be written once and found by everyone who needs it.

New terms: knowledge base, article template, draft, publish, version, valid to date.

## Before you start

- You finished [episode 09](../09-approval). Priya Nair exists.
- Sign in as `admin`.

## Steps

1. Open the All menu, type `Knowledge`, and under Knowledge choose Articles, then Create New.
2. A page asks you to select a knowledge base, a labelled shelf that holds articles, each with its own rules about who can read it. The list shows KCS Knowledge Base, Known Error, IT and Knowledge. Choose `Knowledge`, the shelf that employees such as Priya are allowed to read and that publishes at once. (The IT base asks for approval first and employees cannot read it.)
3. Choose the template `Standard` and click Next.
4. The form opens with a Number already filled in (KB0010002 in the video). Fill it in:

   | Field | Value |
   |---|---|
   | Short description | Fix Wi-Fi on Arcwell laptops |
   | Knowledge base | Knowledge |

   Leave Category empty and leave Valid to at its default (2100-01-01).
5. In the article body type three numbered steps:

   1. Forget the Arcwell Wi-Fi network on the laptop.
   2. Turn Wi-Fi off, wait ten seconds, and turn it on.
   3. Reconnect with the Arcwell company profile.

6. Click Save. The header reads the article number with version 0.01, and the Publish button appears. A draft is not visible to readers.
7. Click Publish. After a short wait a message says the knowledge article has been published and the version is 1.0.
8. Impersonate Priya Nair from the avatar menu.
9. Priya's view opens on the Knowledge page. It says Welcome to Knowledge and counts 1 Knowledge Bases and 1 Articles.
10. In the search box type the exact title `Fix Wi-Fi on Arcwell laptops` and click the search icon. The page shows 1 result, your article. Then try the shorter word `wifi` and look at what comes back: in the video it returned no results. Search the exact title again.
11. Open the article from the result. It shows the number, the title, who authored it and the three steps.
12. Open the menu under Priya's avatar and choose End Impersonation.

Result: the article is published as version 1.0 and Priya finds it by its exact title.

## Your turn

1. Write your own short article in the Knowledge base with the title `PRACTICE Order a replacement laptop`, and publish it.
2. Impersonate Priya and find it by its exact title.
3. Search for one word from its title and see what comes back.
4. Remove only your practice article, and keep the Wi-Fi article.

## Clean up

End the impersonation. Delete (or retire and delete) the practice article. Keep `Fix Wi-Fi on Arcwell laptops`.
